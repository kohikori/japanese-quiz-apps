Backup & sync

Your progress is saved on each device separately. To move it between your phone and laptop, back up on one and restore on the other. Restoring merges results, so nothing gets overwritten. Do it in both directions and both devices end up with everything.

On your other device
Share backup opens your phone's share sheet: AirDrop, Messages, email or Save to Files. Copy sync code is the same backup as text, handy for pasting into a note or a message to yourself.
